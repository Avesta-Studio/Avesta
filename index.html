Official Studio Policies & Terms

DYNAST Horology Warranty & Bespoke Crafting Policies

Mandatory Buyer Terms
1. Strictly No Replacement on Water or Physical Damage

Every DYNAST timepiece is inspected prior to dispatch. However, strictly no warranty, free repair, or replacement is provided for water damage, moisture ingress, steam exposure, or physical/accidental damage (including dropped watches, cracked sapphire/mineral glass, bent crowns, scratched PVD coatings, or detached links after use).

2. Custom-Printed Dials & Personalized Orders

Because your timepiece is hand-assembled to your exact specifications—and dials with custom names or custom photographs are permanently printed exclusively for you—all bespoke orders are final sale and non-refundable once dial printing and assembly begin.

3. Compulsory 360° Parcel Unboxing Video

In the rare event of transit damage during courier delivery, customers must provide a continuous, unedited 360° unboxing video showing the sealed shipping label and package opening within 24 hours of delivery. Claims without an original unboxing video cannot be processed.

4. Hand-Assembly Timeline & Photo Privacy

Bespoke watches require 4 to 7 business days for custom dial printing, acrylic sealing, movement regulation, and quality testing in Agra before dispatch. All uploaded customer dial photographs are kept strictly confidential and used solely for printing your dial.
