Official Studio Policies & Terms

DYNAST Horology Warranty & Bespoke Crafting Policies

Mandatory Buyer Terms
1. Strictly No Replacement on Water or Physical Damage

Every DYNAST timepiece is tested prior to dispatch. However, strictly no warranty, free repair, or replacement is provided for water damage, moisture ingress, or physical/accidental damage (drops, cracked glass, bent crowns, or scratched cases).

2. Custom-Printed Dials & Personalized Orders

Because dials with custom names or custom photographs are permanently printed exclusively for you, all bespoke orders are final sale and non-refundable once printing and assembly begin.

3. Compulsory 360° Parcel Unboxing Video

For any transit issue during courier delivery, customers must provide a continuous, unedited 360° unboxing video showing the sealed shipping label within 24 hours of delivery.

4. Hand-Assembly Timeline & Tracking

Bespoke watches require 4 to 7 business days for custom dial printing, sealing, and regulation in Agra before dispatch. A live Tracking ID is emailed immediately upon order placement.
